ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION

WE ARE THE MUSUAN TITANS.

The Musuan Titans Esports Guild is the official competitive esports student organization of Central Mindanao University.

Named after the iconic Musuan Peak overlooking the CMU campus, the organization brings together talented student gamers who represent the university in collegiate esports competitions.

The Guild aims to develop esports leadership, digital literacy, teamwork, and sportsmanship among CMU students.
