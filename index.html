ABOUT THE TITANS

The Musuan Titans Esports Guild is a university esports organization dedicated to bringing together passionate student gamers of Central Mindanao University.

Through teamwork, discipline, and competitive excellence, the guild aims to develop student talent and represent the university in esports competitions.
